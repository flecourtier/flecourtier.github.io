Explore an interactive map showing the geographic locations where I've presented talks and posters. Use the legend on the map to filter between talks (🔴 red) and posters (🔵 blue), or click on any marker to see the event details.

Tips: Zoom in/out to explore different regions, hover over clusters to see the number of events, and use the checkboxes to show or hide specific types of events.
